About Me

I am a mathematics Ph.D. student at Tel Aviv University under the supervision of Professor Leonid Polterovich and Professor Yael Karshon. My field of research is symplectic geometry. Currently, I am mainly interested in symplectic torus actions and methods of generating functions.
